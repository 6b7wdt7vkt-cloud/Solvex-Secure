Solar-Powered Surveillance

Visibility that doesn't depend on the grid.

A solar surveillance system combines solar panels, battery storage, cameras and connectivity into one independent installation. The panels charge the batteries during the day and the batteries run the system through the night.

Well suited to

  • Farms and agricultural properties
  • Construction sites
  • Remote properties
  • Equipment yards
  • Temporary sites
  • Areas where running mains electricity is difficult
